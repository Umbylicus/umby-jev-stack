Highlighting

Highlight posts

Gold outline when a post matches an interest. Red outline and a red X for a non-interest, an ad, or obvious spam.

Pick broad topics. Interested posts stay. Not interested posts are the ones Hide posts can remove.

Social

Ads

A sponsored or promoted post is an ad. Remove that card. If removal would delete a real post, blur it completely.

Hide posts

Delete not-interested posts, spam, and ads from the page as they appear. If a block cannot be removed, blur it. Mail is never deleted. Spam on social sites is removed even when this is off.

Focus mode

Interest posts stay full size. Everything else is blurred. With Hide posts on, everything except an interest is removed when the page allows it. Trends, Shorts, Reels, and extra rails hide.

        Mail

        Mail highlight

        In Gmail and Outlook, interest mail is gold. Junk, spam, and a non-interest get a red line through the subject. Mail is not deleted.

        Page tools

        Draft check

        In a compose box, mark a sentence that promises a price, a date, or a refund. Nothing is sent.

        Checkout domain

        On a pay page, flag when the header brand does not match the domain.

        Steps only

        On a tutorial, keep the numbered steps and collapse the essay above them.

        Reject cookies

        Outline the reject or essential-only button. It is not clicked.

        Decision line

        Select a paragraph for one line with the decision, the owner, and the date.

        Must-haves

        On a product page, mark a spec row that fails a requirement you typed.

        PDF amounts and signatures

        In a PDF, outline amounts and signature lines.

        Job fit

        On a job post, what you can do is gold and a missed requirement is red.

        Help me cancel

        Show a Help me cancel button that glows the next cancel control. It does not click.

        Answer jump

        Type a question about the open article and jump to the section that answers it.

        Duplicate site

        If this URL is already open, ignoring tracking parameters, show a card.

        Documents

        Terms card

        On a terms or privacy page, list auto-renew, the cancellation window, arbitration, and what data is sold.

        Proposal diff

        With two proposals or contracts open, list clauses where price, term, or liability changed.
